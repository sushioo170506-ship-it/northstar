# Research Report Authoring

Turn the synthesized analysis into a polished, decision-grade deliverable
tailored to the audience and decision from `decision-framing`.

## Inputs
- The scored fit matrix, weighted comparison, and positioning from
  `comparison-synthesis`/`competitive-positioning`; all Phase C pillar outputs.

## Bundled assets
- `templates/report-template.md` — the report skeleton (start here).
- `templates/comparison-table.md` — weighted comparison + fit matrix format.
- `templates/tco-model.md` — cost/TCO scenario table.
- `references/credibility-rubric.md` — A–D tiers + confidence labels (include a legend).
- `references/report-quality-checklist.md` — the definition of done; self-audit against it.

## Steps
1. **Start from the template** and fill all sections.
2. **Write two layers:**
   - **Executive summary** (decision-makers): the recommendation, weighted verdict,
     top findings, top risks/unknowns, and the pilot plan — jargon-light.
   - **Detailed findings** (technical): capability profile, first-hand results,
     failure modes, TCO, operational readiness, risk register, positioning, with
     the comparison table and fit matrix.
3. **Make the recommendation conditional and actionable:** what to do, under what
   preconditions, what would flip it, plus a **pilot plan** (what to test next,
   success metrics) and **guardrails** for safe adoption.
4. **Annotate every key claim** with credibility tier + confidence + date/version;
   include the legend.
5. **Give failure modes and known unknowns their own sections** — do not bury them.
6. **Flag every vendor-supplied number**; separate Fact from Assessment.
7. **Include the TCO model** and the weighted comparison, not just prose.
8. **Disclose methodology** — what was researched/tested and what was not (for
   trust and reproducibility).
9. **Stamp temporal validity**: "information current as of <date>", a re-check /
   expiry date, and a **watch-list** of things that would change the verdict.
10. **Match the output language** to the user's request.
11. **Self-audit against `report-quality-checklist.md`** before delivering.

## Definition of done
Every item in `references/report-quality-checklist.md` passes.
